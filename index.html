❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Ecosystem Clarification

Automatic and manual withdrawal simultaneously via global Chainlink Oracle decentralized network +
When the investment plan reaches completion, direct and immediate withdrawal is executed automatically and instantly by the user. Due to potential network or Chainlink cross-chain delays, if a user experiences any delay in withdrawal, the system provides a second method: automated transfer via decentralized Chainlink contracts. Furthermore, users can manually withdraw instantly upon plan completion to have their capital and plan profit transferred immediately and smoothly.
Smart contract permissions system for investment and trading +
Our decentralized system relies on the official Bank Swap code without proxies, bots, or monitoring servers to oversee investments. To ensure absolute decentralization, when a user initiates an investment or trading session, they must grant permission to the contract to withdraw the user's funds for a specific amount entered by the user. The user signs, and signs again to execute the investment immediately, withdrawing everything to guarantee 100% decentralization. Everything is transparent so that the user knows they are interacting with a contract that contains no proxy or owner, operating independently and permanently forever.
Does the system accept manual or direct transfers to the decentralized contract? +
There are no external direct receiving functions in the contract to prevent fake transactions and re-entrancy vulnerabilities. Any direct manual transfer is strictly rejected; funds must be processed exclusively through the investment functions or trading section for user safety and to eliminate re-entrancy exploits. Everything is completely transparent and verifiable in the contract code.
What is the nature of the investment and profit system? +
Automated investment designed with the highest security standards with stable interest up to 30% monthly. The invested amount is deducted and traded securely inside a liquidity pool, guaranteeing risk-free trading profits based on analyses from the world's largest exchange data source Chainlink Data, with charts powered by TradingView. Real trading source verified through USDT transactions routed automatically and returning safely to the user via Uniswap liquidity pools.
What happens when a user withdrawal is requested? +
If the user has an active referral upline, the capital is refunded and only the exact percentage of profits that were distributed to the upline is deducted. If the user joined without any referral upline, 100% of the full capital is refunded with zero deductions. Upon plan completion, direct withdrawal by the user is immediate and automatic, while Chainlink decentralized contracts serve as a reliable backup method for automatic transfers in case of any delays.
When are profits added to my wallet? +
At the end of the investment period, direct and immediate withdrawal can be executed by the user instantly. If any delay occurs, automated transfers are processed instantly via decentralized Chainlink contracts as a second secure method.
